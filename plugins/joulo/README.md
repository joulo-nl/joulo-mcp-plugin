# Joulo

Joulo is a Dutch service that pays electric car drivers for the electricity they charge at home, through ERE (Emissiereductie-eenheden). This plugin connects your AI assistant to your own Joulo account. Ask about your charging stations, sessions, energy totals, electricity prices, charging costs and ERE position. The assistant answers from your own data. It also finds public chargers in the Netherlands, near a place or along a route.

## What you can ask

- Which of my charging stations count for ERE, and is one charging now?
- How much energy did I charge yesterday? Show my kWh per month next to last year.
- When is electricity cheapest tonight to charge my car?
- What did charging at home cost me last month, and what does a kilometre cost me?
- For 2026, how much of my ERE is expected, reserved, traded, payable and paid?
- Where can I charge near Utrecht Centraal, and what does a session cost there?
- Where can I fast-charge on the way from Utrecht to Groningen?
- Mail me when a laadpunt at that location comes free.

Session euro values and the indicative ERE price are estimates, not guaranteed payouts. Public charging prices are the prices the operator publishes; a charge card can cost more or less.

## What it can change

One thing: a mail alert for a public charging location. The assistant turns one on only after you say yes. It mails your Joulo address once when a laadpunt comes free, and you can stop it again. Everything else only reads.

The plugin cannot start or stop charging, change your account, delete data, sell ERE's or start a payment.

## Setup

You need a Joulo account (https://joulo.nl). After you install the plugin, run `/mcp`, choose `plugin:joulo:joulo` and sign in with your Joulo account. You choose which permissions to grant, and you can revoke them in your Joulo dashboard.
